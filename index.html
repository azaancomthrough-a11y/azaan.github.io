THE TIMELINE

Football 2000–2026

A quick journey through the modern football era.

2000–2005

A new generation began to emerge while stars such as Zidane, Ronaldo, Henry and Ronaldinho dominated the game.

2006–2010

Barcelona's golden generation developed alongside major World Cup stories from Italy and Spain.

2011–2015

Messi, Ronaldo, Xavi, Iniesta and Neymar became central figures in one of football's most memorable eras.

2016–2019

Portugal won Euro 2016, France won the 2018 World Cup, and a new generation began challenging the established stars.

2020–2022

Lewandowski, Haaland, Mbappé and other stars rose while Argentina's 2022 World Cup triumph became a historic moment.

2023–2026

Football entered another changing generation, with young talents such as Lamine Yamal joining established global stars on the biggest stage.

Football Never Stops. ⚽
